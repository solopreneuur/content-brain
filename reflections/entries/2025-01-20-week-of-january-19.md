### Key Opinions & Advice

1.  **Small Time Windows for SEO Gains**
      
      - 6 weeks is short for major Google ranking changes. That you’ve seen impressions at all is positive. The daily backlink push is crucial—**quality** matters.
2.  **Customer Discovery Is Golden**
      
      - If owners express that a directory is “nice to have” but not truly needed, you might pivot to micro tools or a more direct solution.
      - If they show enthusiasm, channel that into your marketing copy: “Built with real restaurant owner feedback.”
3.  **Pace Yourself**
      
      - 5 backlinks/day plus 2–3 calls per week plus content updates is a **lot** for a solo effort. If you see it’s too much, **prioritize**: (1) interviews, (2) consistent SEO improvements, and (3) micro tool building.
4.  **Don’t Overlook Direct Engagement**
      
      - Guest posting is great, but also consider **restaurant forums**, local small business groups, or LinkedIn groups to share your directory. Personal outreach can yield high-quality links or referral traffic.
