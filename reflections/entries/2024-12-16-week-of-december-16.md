## Roadmap:

#### **Week of December 9: Understand the Industry (Step 1)** ✅

  - Spend **1 week** learning the basics of the restaurant industry.
  - Focus on:
      
      - **Core Insights**: Understand the types of restaurants (fast food, fine dining, franchises) and the tools they use (e.g., POS systems, delivery apps, inventory management).
      - **Problem Areas**: Explore forums (Reddit: r/kitchenconfidential, r/smallbusiness), online reviews (Yelp, Google), and blogs to identify recurring issues.
      - **Key Metrics/Terms**: Learn what matters to restaurant owners (e.g., food costs, labor costs, profit margins).
  - **Checkpoint for Completion**:
      
      - List 5-10 broad challenges restaurant owners face. ✅
      - Be confident enough to hold meaningful conversations in interviews. ✅
      - Identify where to find real problems (forums, reviews, tools). ✅

-----

#### **Week of December 16: Problem Identification (Steps 2 and 3)**

  - **Step 2: Compile a List of 50-100 Problems**
      
      - Use insights from week 1 to identify potential issues.
      - Sources: Online forums, reviews, blogs, and conversations.
      - Organize the problems into a structured list.
  - **Step 3: Assess Problems with Keyword Research**
      
      - Validate demand for solutions by analyzing search volume and trends.
      - Prioritize problems with high search interest or recurring complaints.
  - **Checkpoint for Completion**:
      
      - A refined list of problems ranked by potential importance and demand.

-----

#### **Week of December 23: Interview Preparation (Step 4)**

  - **Step 4: Create a List of Questions for Interviews**
      
      - Develop targeted questions to uncover:
          
          - Pain points in daily operations.
          - Current solutions they’re using (and their limitations).
          - Willingness to pay for solutions.
      - Questions should focus on refining your problem list further.
  - **Checkpoint for Completion**:
      
      - A clear, structured set of interview questions ready for use.
      - Clear list of people to talk to

-----

#### **Week of December 30 (or January 6): Customer Discovery (Steps 5 and 6)**

  - **Step 5: Conduct Interviews**
      
      - Whip up CRM
      - Talk to10 **restaurant owners** in each category to validate and refine your problem list.
      - Record key insights and add to CRM and identify recurring themes.
      - Cross-reference interview findings with your keyword research from step 3.
  - **Step 6: Pick a Promising Problem**
      
      - Use all the gathered data to select a high-potential problem.
      - Criteria:
          
          - Clear customer pain point.
          - Evidence of willingness to pay.
          - Potential for a scalable solution.
  - **Checkpoint for Completion**:
      
      - A validated problem you’re confident pursuing.

-----
