## PART 1: Ideal Viewer Persona

Age: 23

**IVP Identity Paragraph**
One paragraph — who are they, what life stage, what do they believe about themselves, what do they secretly doubt?

My ideal viewer is a 21 year-old internet native male who wants to become an entrepreneur and loves self improvement. I’m reasonably smart, social, ambitious, and proud of how much I’ve figured out on my own — but I know I’m not a genius. I genuinely believe I can build a successful life but I keep having moments where I question myself, I keep seeing people online and people I went to college with getting ahead of me, they are making the leap to be a full time founder or have started making money online and I pride myself in my ability and my intelligence but I can’t seem to figure out what I’m doing wrong. I try to spend time outside my job trying to figure out how to make money outside of it, but I have no idea where to start and I have issues with staying disciplined on getting much work done outside of my job.

I work hard, but I’m almost never satisfied with how hard I work because other people seem to get results faster. That comparison makes me question my intelligence, my potential, and sometimes my own self-worth. Because of that, I crave proof that I’m actually as capable as I believe I am.
I don’t believe my background dooms me. I believe I can win.
I just can’t figure out why some people seem to move through life so much faster than I do — or what they know that I don’t.






**Biggest Pain Points (aim for 10)**
Specific enough that "I already know this" is impossible.

I try my hardest to work on business/startup stuff outside my job but I’m moving incredibly slow and haven’t done anything real or tangible.
I get constantly distracted with how to prioritize, I want to lock in and start a company but travel and see the world in my 20s but also be healthy and lift weights. Many times I chose hanging out with my friends because it's “an experience I can’t miss” instead of spending that time working.
All of my friends around me are just working their job and it feels like I’m the only person that wants to do more to do something outside of it. I see people online but I don’t know any in person.
I don’t know how to come up with ideas or what I should even be building for a startup. I’m an engineer I’m not “the idea guy”
I think I need to quit my 9-5 and pursue a startup full time but because I grew up in poverty and I’m afraid to disappoint my brown parents I haven’t taken the leap.
I have a hard time networking with rich white men, I know it's important because they dominate the investing and mentoring landscape, and I’m not a weirdo but I just find it hard as a minority to connect with older white people.
I want to find love and get married but I feel like a relationship will hinder me from being able to start a business because it is time consuming so I’m stuck in paralysis when it comes to women.
I’m a dopamine addict, I restrict myself really hard for a few weeks and I do great and hit my goals and then one setback and I skip working time, workouts and start smoking, binge eating and hanging out with my friends.
On the surface I am a confident person but I actually attach a lot of my self worth to external things like the approval from how successful I am and women.
I hold the belief that I am different from others but still feel stuck.
