Hear me out. 

The Hip hop fan to entrepreneur arc is the most predictable life path that exists on Earth right now. 

It goes something like this: At around 15 or 16 years old, you start listening to rap, you probably start with people like Kanye or Kendrick. 

You fall in love with the music but even more so with the message behind it. 

You go on genius and start reading the breakdowns of the lyrics and see the messages behind these albums. 

You keep seeing the same things, being different, believing in yourself, making it out.

And you see that the success of these artists all came from working relentlessly toward their dream and realize you want to do the same.

As a result you start working on yourself, you starting lifting, learning how to dress and start developing real confidence in yourself. 

And once this happens, you starting thinking about the bigger priorities in life, like what career to pursue and how to make money. 

But at this point, you start realizing that there’s no room for a dream in the 9-5.

There’s no creativity, no real journey in “climbing the ladder” and it’s what everyone else does. 

And so at this moment, a switch flips on in your head where you realize, “Shit, I need to build my own thing." 

And just like that, your entrepreneurial journey has begun. If you see this video and this sounds like you, hi, I'm Huzaifa, and you just found your people.
