10/10 signs that the person you're talking to is extremely resourceful but it gets increasingly more niche


On screen: think on the spot
They force themselves into situations where they have to think on the spot.
Things like cold calling and cold approaching train your brain to collapse into certainty.
This is a skill that pops up in every domain of life whether its a first date or a big meeting at your company
And contrary to popular belief it’s a skill that can be trained with enough reps. 

On screen: unorthodox inspiration
They pull inspiration from unorthodox places.
Originality is just how far the distance is between the two things you combine.
That’s why all the greatest minds of history were polymaths. (Pictures of galleleo, Picasso etc)

On screen: shift the paradigm
And finally they constantly shift the paradigm.

When dealing with a new domain instead of optimizing they ask the question is this even the right thing to be doing.

This is called double loop thinking, instead of wasting months stuck on a problem often times it’s just the wrong problem to be solving.
