Hear me out.

The cracked gamer to entrepreneur arc is the most predictable life path on Earth right now.

It goes something like this, at around age 12 or 13 you start playing video games, you probably start with Black ops 2 or Gta 5.

Everyday after school you get off the bus and turn on your xbox and hop on with your friends, and you start getting insanely good. 

You get addicted to getting chopper gunners and k9 units game after game.

And As a result you start chasing progress in real life, so you start lifting weights, learning how to dress better, and begin to develop real confidence in yourself. 

And once this happens, you starting thinking about the bigger priorities in life, like what career to pursue and how to make money. 

But at this point you realize that there’s no real progress in the 9-5.

Your fate rests in your managers hands and making money is painfully slow compared to the progress you’ve experienced in the past.

And so at this moment, a switch flips on in your head where you realize, “Shit, I need to build my own thing." 

And just like that, your entrepreneurial journey has begun. If you see this video and this sounds like you, hi, I'm Huzaifa, and you just found your people.
