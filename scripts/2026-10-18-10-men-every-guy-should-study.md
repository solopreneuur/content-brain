These are 10 men every guy in his twenties should study. 
 
Number one, Cristiano Ronaldo. Living proof that maintaining greatness requires more discipline than achieving it. 

Number two  Leonardo da Vinci. He refused to become a master of one.  Art, science, engineering, anatomy. He mastered each one, just one at a time.

Number 3 Marcus Aurelius He teaches that you have control over your mind, not outside events.

Number 4 Kanye West. He entered the rap industry in a time where you had to be a thug then flipped the entire concept of being a rapper through sheer self belief.

Number 5 Muhammed (PBUH). He proved that even when the world is calling you mad, having unyielding belief in your message can change the course of history.

Number 6 Beethoven. He lost his hearing the very sense his craft depended on yet kept composing masterpieces—proof that mastery means creating even when your circumstances stop cooperating.

Number 7 Floyd Mayweather He understood that defense, patience, and preparation can make someone nearly impossible to defeat.

Number 8 Michael Jordan He became the greatest basketball player in history by turning competitiveness into a weapon, treating every loss as fuel to raise his standard.

Number 9 Lee Kuan Yew He understood that nations, like people, are shaped by standards. He turned Singapore into a global powerhouse by obsessing over competence, long-term thinking, and refusing to tolerate mediocrity.

Number ten, your future self the one who despite imposter syndrome and feeling unmotivated showed up for years and built and empire. 

And don’t just memorize these peoples quotes. 

Study their habits mindset and the prices they paid for mastery and apply it directly to your life by having recurring reminders.
