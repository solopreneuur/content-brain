Let me give you a hypothetical situation.

What if Graham Bell the guy behind the telephone was given claude

I think that he would have went to claude and been like, “Hey is it possible to speak into a box and someone 1000s of miles away from me hears what I said in another box”?

And claude would say “we have collected all the research and scientific evidence up until this point, until the 1800s”

And have concluded that it is impossible to talk into a box and have someone 1000 miles away hear you. 

And I don’t know what Graham Bell would have done, maybe he would have continued teaching deaf kids.

But of course he defied all conventional wisdom at the time, and innovated, iterated and changed the course of history.

As an entrepreneur and innovator today the second most powerful skill is knowing how to use AI, the first is knowing the difference between where it excels and where it’s a bottleneck.
