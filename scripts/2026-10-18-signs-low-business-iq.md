10/10 signs that the person you’re talking to has low business IQ but it get’s increasingly more niche.


One: They call themself the idea guy

When Zuckerberg founded Facebook in 2004 everyone had the exact same idea 

so then why is no one using MySpace anymore?

Well its because Myspace literally added extra clicks just so that a user would see more ads.

Whereas Facebook focused on less clicks thinking about what made it easier for their user

Same idea, better execution 

Two: They’re obsessed with raising money.

Every time you talk to this guy they’re talking about Vcs and pre seed funding.

The biggest companies in the world Apple, Disney, Microsoft, Oracle, none of them took on venture capital to start. 

All of them however, did validate their product by getting real paying customers.

If you don’t have proven demand fundraising is a waste of time.

Three: They quit their full time job “to go all in”

Steve Wozniak, Phil Knight, Sara Blakey and countless others all kept their jobs before they went full time. 

Phil Knight quit Nike 5 years in after making $150,000 because his time finally became a problem that kept the company from growing. 

Until you have actual proof that your time itself is the bottleneck and not that your product just sucks, there’s no point in quitting.

All 3 of these things make you “feel like an entrepreneur” instead of actually being one.
