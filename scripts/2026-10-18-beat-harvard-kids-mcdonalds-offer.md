The biggest lie we’ve been told as kids of immigrants just work harder.


Being in stem humbled me fast because I would be sitting there STRUGGLING on my HW and pranjeet over here finished it 2 weeks ago!? (How can I word this better?)

So naturally when it came time to apply to jobs the anxiety was real, I have to compete with these people (change to past continuous if possible)

But seeing my mom work 3 jobs I wasn’t gonna just sit there and do nothing

so every day I was sitting in my library applying, I sent out 175 applications.

And every day I saw the same email from a different company -> “we’re sorry to inform you blah blah blah”

Until 1 day, a company I lowkey applied to as a joke hit me back, McDonald’s (para papapappa).

But then I saw how much they paid and that the headquarters is IN CHICAGO

It was literally everything I was looking for.

But then I saw the invite for the interview, THREE HOURS!?

And then I saw the date, it was the same day I had a meeting for my startup AND a volleyball tournament. (CORTISOL SPIKE)

But SO WHAT,

The day the interview came I was ready, I had a yellow tie, yellow lights in the background and a big can of red bull. 

The first half was a group call and as everyone was introducing themself I was like, yeah, I am cooked. 

Harvard, MIT, Northwestern etc. Damn yall wanna put the fries in the bag that badly?

But again, Im not one to back down, so once they asked us if we had any questions I went to town. 

“Whats your 5 year vision at the company, why McDonald’s is it because of the scale” etc

But then we had the 1:1 section and the first guy I got paired up with was one of those monotone whites, I did the best I could.

My next interviewer though was this girl with a hijab and right away I knew that she was Palestentian. 

See I know ball, so I asked her things like oh what village are you from in Palestine, oh my friend actually has the same last name as you, is that your cousin?

After the 1:1s we got brought back into the group call and I had to ask Harvard boy, yo how did you think it went, and just by voice I knew he was not confident.

That was nice but I still had some doubt about that 1 white dude interviewer. 

I put my phone on DND and gunned it to my startup meeting.

The whole time during that meeting all I could think about was the interview.

But, 60 minutes later I took my phone off DND first thing I saw was “congratulations, on your offer”

They gave me the offer the SAME day, they were THAT confident. 

You see everything I did in that interview was intentional, the tie, the questions, relating to the interviewer all of it. 

This is like 1 of 50 tricks I do to get everything I want, if you want to learn more of these I got a whole bag full of them, follow along.
