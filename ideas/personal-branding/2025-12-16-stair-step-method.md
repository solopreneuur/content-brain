# Stair step method

So many things in life so many daunting tasks can become so much easier when you break them down into the smallest steps possible. If I told you to cure cancer I'm just using an extreme example to demonstrate you're gonna be like wtf I don't know how to cure cancer. Here's where the stair step method comes in. You start at the first step to do that, in this case it would probably be find out what the qualifications are for the people doing cancer research. You can figure that out in 2 minutes with chat gpt. Then you go to the next step okay how do I get these qualifications, etc etc etc. Eventually you have a phd and you're in a lab working on curing cancer and one day 40 years from now you crack it. Now obviously curing cancer is an extreme example but the point is that daunting tasks can feel so much more doable when you break them into a bunch of small easy steps.
