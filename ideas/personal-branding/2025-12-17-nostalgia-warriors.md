# Nostalgia Warriors

If all your friends talk about is the past it's a sign you should make some more new friends. Now I'm not saying switch up on your day 1s I don't agree w that at all. But who you spend your time with is who you become. If you are looking forward and trying to succeed and have big goals it's good to be around people like that (this is obvious asl). When people only talk about the past they are what I like to call a nostalgia warrior. They are so caught up with romanticizing the past that they don't even live in the current moment and let alone think about th future. The worst part is half the time the romanticizing is just of something that wasn't all that they make it seem like because the Tim you're currently in now they might hate their life and 5 years later they'll just be back to romanticizing this time. You can never move forward if all you do is look back. Look back as a lesson, Cherish the good times the memories but live in the present and look forward.
