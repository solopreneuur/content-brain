# Writers block

What really is writers block its you thinking you are stuck kn your creative process.

There can be many causes but for me personally I've noticed it's actually when I try to think too hard.

Creativity isn't some algorithm it's a bit more abstract.

Thing is there's nothing wrong your brain is just in a cooldown period.

The thing that worked best for me is taking a step away an intentional break could be an hour or a few days, and at some point on my "break" I just get hit with inspiration. It's because our brains do a lot of background processing subconsciously and eventually it erects (please don't say this) in your conscious brain.
