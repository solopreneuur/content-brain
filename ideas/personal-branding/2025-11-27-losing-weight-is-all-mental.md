# Losing Weight is all mental

When you set out to lose weight the hard truth is you can never go back to how it was before you started. You will have an identity shift and you will change forever. Your relationship with food will change. Now I'm not saying you will eat salads and fruits for the rest of your life. BUT you can never go back to a place where you're regularly binging and most of your diet is shit processed foods. You will still be able to eat what you like it's just more controlled and in a way where you actually feel good regularly. You body literally starts rejecting and feeling shitty when you've been cutting for a while and switch back to eating super dirty it goes wtf are you doing and it just makes u feel sluggish and ass.
