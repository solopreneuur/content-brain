# Confidence Keeping Promises to Yourself

I'm a firm believer in the fact that a big part of confidence stems from keeping promises to yourself. What is the definition of confidence, you have belief in your abilities. Now think about it our brains are like computers we use previous data to make future decisions. (Say simpler). If your brain has always seen you time after time again break promises to yourself why would it think that okay this time is going to be different. Now I'm not saying you're fucked if you've broken promises repeatedly to yourself I mean that's being human we all do it. But what you need to do is give your brain new data with your new actions. So when you say you're going to do something do it. When you show up day after day even if you've messed up if on average even on the small things you are doing what you said, how will you not end up developing self belief?
