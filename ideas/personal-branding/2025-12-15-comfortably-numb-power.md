# Comfortably Numb/power

The biggest villain stopping people from becoming who they truly are are two things.

Fear of failure

Comfort.

Now we've all heard this over and over again. But the thing is these two things are really rooted in the fact that when your life is set up in a way where not making a change doesn't have consequences it is so much harder for your brain to justify making a leap. That's why a lot of hyper successful people you see come from very hard life stories like the guy who lived off a generator or _____.

Now now all of us have crazy tragic backstories (thankfully) nor have a small loan of a million dollars. And the environment that's set up is as long as you do the bare minimum you will survive and see tomorrow.

This is where the power of being backed into a corner comes in. Sometimes it is actually better for you to make a risky decision knowing you might end up in a worse spot if it doesn't work. But someone's worse situations are better than comfortably numb situations they enable that action potential and spring you out of it.

Reminds me of a friend who everyone always called dumb, he bought a $60k car at 22 with a minimum wage job. The thing that stuck with me about him and the smartest thing I ever heard him say was, it makes me grind harder. The power of the corner. And he was right a few years later he got a much higher paying job and paid it off.
