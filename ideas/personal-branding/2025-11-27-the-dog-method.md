# The Dog Method

One of the things people struggle a lot with when trying to cut is what to eat. I always talk about the dog method, finding 3-5 foundational things you really like and can tolerate having everyday. It takes off so much mental load and ensures you get what you need to in terms of protein and nutrients. I personally do 4 eggs with spinach high protein bread. 2 Greek yogurts a banana and blueberries every single day. This builds a foundation of protein healthy fats and nutrients from the fruits and veggies. It makes the rest of the day so much easier because I can flex and fit around so many different things.
