# Walking around with stupid amount of confidence

There's this idea of 'fake it till you make it,' and people usually take it the wrong way."  "It's not about pretending to be someone you're not. It's about understanding that confidence is a social signal."  "When you walk around with confidence, people treat you like you're capable. They trust you more. They give you responsibility."  "And eventually, you start growing into the version of you they already see."  "Confidence doesn't just come from ability. Sometimes ability follows confidence."
