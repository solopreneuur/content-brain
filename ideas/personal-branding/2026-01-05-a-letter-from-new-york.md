# 2 months ago I left my life in Chicago and moved to NYC

As I start this new life I thought I would do a little monologue

I wanted to talk about the nature of change

There's two real guarantees in life

Death and change

The life we're all living now won't last forever for better or for worse, hopefully for better

In the coming years jobs will change new relationships will be formed old will be broken some of us will get married others will have kids

Change happens constantly sometimes it's small other times it's life changing, either way, it's inevitable

That doesn't mean it's something to be afraid of, quite the contrary it's exciting, it's truly existing

The point I'm trying to make is that if it's inevitable we should embrace it and make the decisions and do the things we *know* will benefit us in the long term

Either way change is going to happen so why not dictate it and shape your life the way you desire
