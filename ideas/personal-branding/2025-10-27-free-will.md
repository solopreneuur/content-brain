# Free Will

People talk about how where someone is often times isn't their fault and this is not a completely false statement. However there's still merit in the action of trying. If it was between a person who tried for years and worked 80 hours a week who failed and never became something and a guy who was a bum who invested in crypto in 2016 I respect the first guy much more. It's not about the money or what you get but who you become.
