# External confidence developing into internal

One thing that helped me during imposter syndrome was other people believing in me before I fully believed in myself."  "I joined a new team recently, and even when I felt unsure, they treated me like I belonged there."  "And because that belief was there, I showed up differently. I prepared more. I pushed through discomfort."  "The goal is still to build confidence internally."  "But external belief can carry you until your internal confidence catches up.
