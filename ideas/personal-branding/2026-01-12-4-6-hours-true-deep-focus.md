# 4-6 hours true deep focus

Are brains like a gas tank everyday. No matter who we are without drugs 4-6 hours is the limit we have of true deep work.

The rest of the time it's not that we aren't working but we can't really tap into that (research)

Maybe point is: prioritizing highest value tasks to start the day or working 12 hours a day is a myth (with nuance) or working 6 hours focused for 6 days is better than "working" for 12 hours 4 days and then burning out for the next 3
