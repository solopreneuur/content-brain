# Making new friends as an adult is not as hard as people say

I went to a commuter school and I would always meet people in class that said they hated it. They would say there's nothing going on. I would always ask them the same question, what do you do after class. They said go home. Well no shit if you're going home after class you're going to hate the school you're entire perception of it is derived from you being in class. The thing is there was stuff gling on and it was everyday there were random meetups and club org events and sporting events it was just a matter of showing up. This same thing applies as an adult but it just takes some more effort. If you're just going home after work or hanging with the same people you've known and doing the same things you've done on weekends how exactly are you going to meet new people it's not like someone is gonna run up to you and be like hey let's be friends. You have to put yourself in the situations where you meet people in social settings. There are volunteering clubs, communities, coworking spaces, events in every interest field you can think of happening around your city. You have to go to these things and if you go regularly there's no reason you won't be able to meet new people and make new friends.
