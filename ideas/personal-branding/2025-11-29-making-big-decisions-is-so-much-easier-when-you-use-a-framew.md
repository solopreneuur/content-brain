# Making big decisions is so much easier when you use a framework

Is there something you need to make a decision on but have been putting of because it's just too much. It probably is too much if you don't navigate it correctly. When making a big decision you can apply a very easy "3 step framework" to it. Write down the decision on a piece of paper and have a side that's no and the other is yes. Under each one put pros and cons. Write those out and under that you can write will I regret this in 5 years. To go further make a table of things that are impkrtsnt to you. I put, fulfillment and monetary gain. Then I rate it out of 5. Do that and both sides and the answer will be so much clearer to you.
