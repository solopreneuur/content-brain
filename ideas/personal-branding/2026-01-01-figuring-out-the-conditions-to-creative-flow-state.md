# Figuring out the conditions to creative flow state

Flow state is something commonly talked about and used a lot more in a physical sense like in sports or f1 driving.

However flow state can be in anything. Creative flow state the opposite of writers block.

If you're a creative you've definitely had a moment or many moments when the ideas flow, the pen is sharp the (insert something)

What I've realized for myself is there are conditions to unlock them

It varies for every person I would think but what works for me are 3 very simple things.

Full night of sleep, a little bit of caffeine and a clear head on a lengthy walk.

Every single time without fail I end up coming up with 10+ video ideas.

Find your conditions and milk them it's not a whim it's something u can engineer into ur life.
