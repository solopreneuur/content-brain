# As a Type A being unorganized for a day

Us Type A's are very organized to the point people. We have schedules and times blocks and to do lists. This is great for us in terms of how today's society works and it helps make it easy for us to navigate (say with nuance so typical b don't get offended). However sometimes we over plan and over do it. Sometimes I think it's actually a good practice to once in a while have a day where you just go with the flow. Now don't do this on a day that has some big thing yk but a day off or a holiday day. I realized this Christmas week when I was off. I had a day where I just had to do some steps spend time with my mom and go to the gym and there I was planning out the day to a T. It was so unnecessary and for the context of that day since my responsibilities were low letting go actually felt so nice I didn't feel like I was on a clock which can happen many times. Now this is just from the standpoint of a pattern interrupt in your life and can help you tune the way you operate day to day I still think planning and time blocking are extremely effective tools and will always use them it's just some days yk just chill they don't need them.
