However, this isn't about that. That hook I used it's literally one from Devon canup, the thing is I don't normally just steal hooks this was an experiment. I saw another kid literally world for word copy this guys entire video pretty much and he got 600k on it.

I wanted to talk about copying. Look I think that copying can help in a lot of different ways it can give u some temporary boosts it can give u a blow up on a video or maybe a few extra clients on a business. But in the big picture copying is just a tool you should use to give u a little edge, if thats the entirety of what you're doing and you have no original angle no special reason for why you should be doing this thing, than I think you should rethink whatever you are doing. Copying is powerful when used correctly not as your entire strategy.
