# Shiny Object Dilemma

Hook: Self-Recognition Hooks

If you keep starting something then switching for something this is for you (something like this)

Something I've struggled with a lot in the past and am still struggling with is the shiny object dilemma. It's something hermozi mentioned that's stuck with me. It's when you find something other people are doing and go this it my big break how I'll make money and then the moment it gets hard you see something else that looks easier and you switch to that and just get stuck in this loop. Example of this is the ai automation agencies. I tried my hand at this earlier this year but I didn't put in the footwork and hours into it that a business like that truly requires. I was trying it out in different spaces and was looking for a space that made sense for me. I thought I had it I landed on recruiting. Then I got inspired to do content because someone told me I spoke eloquently and it made me realize the thing I do best is yapping. I remember thinking wait slow down there you are  again with the shiny object dillema. So what I did instead of jumping to it is continued to drudge through the work that had gotten hard and annoying with the agency but didn't let go of content as a thought. After months of this thing still bugging me and deep reflection I decided I just have to try it. The thing that made me certain much more than my previous experiences of shiny object dilemma was this time I thought about defensibility. I always think about my friends and how I could put them on once k make it but here's the thing this time I couldn't figure out a way to do it. Because in content what I'm doing is no replicable at least not 1:1 I can't teach someone to be me so when I get bigger I can't teach that. But that's the thing that made me realize this isn't shiny object dilemma but in reality me being suited for this. That's the lesson here to find out what you should really be doing to become successful you need to think about why me what advantage do I have here that only I have. In sass I was an okay founder and okay businessman 1 of 100,000 but in content there's only one me given that I'm a certified yapper I felt it was the right move.
