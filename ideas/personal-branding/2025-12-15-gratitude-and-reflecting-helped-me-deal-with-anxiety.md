# Gratitude and reflecting helped me deal with anxiety

It's 2023 and I am about to graduate college. A couple months prior I had a serious of unfortunate events which caused me to experience anxiety for the first time in my life at least general persistent anxiety. Those events combined with life uncertainty of what life willl look like after college just amplified it. One of my strengths but sometimes it can be a weakness is I am very problem solving oriented. I saw it as a problem something that needed to be fixed. This was only partially correct. The reason I say this is because one thing that couldn't be fixed was time, I needed to graduate and go into the real world there was nothing I could do to accelerate that accept be patient and let it happen. However the thing that did help me relive the symptoms while I was bound by time was practicing gratitude and reflecting. That's when I really developed the habit of thinking more deeply about my life. Everyday I would wake up and think about all the things I had to be grateful for, a family my friends etc. I then used to and still reflect weekly on everything going on my life. The combination of these things gave me clarity and let me at least somewhat whether the storm. Once I graduated and adjusted to real life it slowly started going away. Now this won't work for everyone esp if you have diagnosed general anxiety or chronic anxiety. This is just worked four me in the phase of my life, regardless I still think gratitude and being mindful/reflecting is something that every single human can benefit from
