# Being able to pursue your passion is something you unlock unless you were born rich

The background I grew up in the only option I thought I had was to get to a point of stability and income as fast as I was able to see. So I went to college got my degree and got a 6 figure job. When I was younger I always said passion was for rich kids and work is work. I think my take as someone older now which might be a bit of a hot take is you unlock the ability to do what you're passionate about. It's like Maslow hierchy of needs right once u get your basic needs covered and you're stable and your family is good it is a much more calculated risk and decision to pursue what you're passionate about. The thing people always fail to mention is that most 18 years old kids don't actually know shit I'm 24 and I'm still trying to figure out exactly what I want to do everyday when I wake up for the next 20 years. So like the whole oh don't do stuff for the money whatever I don't agree with. You get some money going which is purely a tool, it forces you to grow and learn because you have to and then you can get to a point where you are older more developed and be able to start venturing out more without worrying about if you're sisters college is paid or your mom is working 3 jobs to pay the bills.
