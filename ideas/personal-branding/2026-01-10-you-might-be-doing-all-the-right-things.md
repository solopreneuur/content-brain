# You might be doing all the right things

Some things just have a warmup phase

For example when u make a fresh TikTok account no matter how perfect and beautiful ur videos are most of the time the algorithm will just be testing ur video to see whose fyp it hits

In this phase u might try over optimizing or changing things too early

But you might actually be doing everything right and stuck in the warmup phase

Even in the gym ur not gonna see abs 2 weeks in even w the right workouts nutrition

The key is looking at the small signs

For example with TikTok u look at if people saved ur video thats a good metric

Or with the gym is the effort to put up the same weight getting slightly easier week by week
