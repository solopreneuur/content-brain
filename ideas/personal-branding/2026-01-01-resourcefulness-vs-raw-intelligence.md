# Resourcefulness vs raw intelligence

One thing I've noticed now being a few years removed from college and a solid amount from high school.

A lot of the people who were super ultra smart that were getting 5s on ap tests and getting mad they got a 89% on an exam are getting beat out in terms of motion (achievement).

I can't put a pin on the archetype exactly but it's people that kind of flew under the radar.

The pattern I've noticed is that now at this age it's people that are resourceful. It's the people that know where to show up who to talk to and honestly biggest thing is using the fuck out of tools they have at their disposal.

It's funny I always used to say in high school why does it matter if I memorize the formula in real life I just need to know how to use it.

That's exactly it.
