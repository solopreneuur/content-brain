# Make friends at the same place in life as you are or above

I'm not saying forget your day ones or (I guess I forgot where I came from Kanye clip) but it is much easier to be able to do things like traveling or even just week to week stuff if you have friends in the same or a better place then you. I bet everyone here that has a 9-5 or a business wants to travel to certain places or do certain things but they can't because their friends are either broke or too busy with school, don't wait for that Japan trip or that life experience because you're waiting for your friend to graduate med school go do it, make new connections while still maintaining the old ones you can always do those things when you're original friends have gotten to the same point or sometimes the truth is those things will never happen with those people and you wasted your time for nothing
