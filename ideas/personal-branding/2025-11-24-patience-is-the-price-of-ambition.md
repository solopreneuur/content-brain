# Patience is the price of Ambition

When you have goals and want to achieve something big often times there are things you want to do in the moment, go one dates, travel, splurge on clothes. But in order to achieve your long term goals some of these things radically slow you down in achieving those so you have to wait sometimes in order to do it. For me it's dating it's hard to date someone with a transitionary period like mine because the partner I find is used to my current lifestyle vs the one I will have once I go full time.
