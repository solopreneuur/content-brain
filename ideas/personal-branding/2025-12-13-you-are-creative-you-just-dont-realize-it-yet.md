# You are creative you just don't realize it yet

The classic "I'm just not a creative person" is bs. You have an entire half of your brain dedicated to creativity. You either haven't found out the outlet you are creative in or haven't created the environment for yourself to be creative. On the first point it's the fact that everyone has different mediums in which they are creative some people are inherently great at music and can hear the notes, others it's art shit even people who are certified cappers even that's creative cause how do you even come up with a lie like that. You need to figure out the medium that works for you. The other half is the fact the age old thing we always hear about nation of workers not thinkers. This is a real thing. If you're in an environment where you are constantly just following directions like school then college then work and you aren't thinking for yourself and don't have anything outside of work or school you are doing how will you ever be creative. That's why finding a medium, and outlet and having times where it's just you in your thoughts is such a powerful tool for the creative process
