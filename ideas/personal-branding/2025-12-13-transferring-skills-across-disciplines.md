# Transferring skills across disciplines

Do you notice some people are just very good at things when they pick them up. Sometimes they may be talented but really it's the muscle memory and practice they've built in other disciplines which on the surface doesn't seem transferable but subconsciously it's what's happening. An example of this is people who were trading crypto during the meme coin revolution a lot of them were top tier gamers it's because gaming allowed them to develop pattern recognition judgment and identifying opportunity in real time and many of them consciously or subconsciously transferred this talent to trading and were wildly successful. I guess the lesson is whatever you are doing even if it doesn't seem like something that will get u closer to your goals you should try hard it'll reap fruits later in ways u had no idea it would.
