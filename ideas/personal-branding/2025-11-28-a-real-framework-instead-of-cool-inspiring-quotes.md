# A real framework instead of cool inspiring quotes

When you come on this platform you see all these creators that tell you things like just keep working till you make it you can't fail if you don't give up. I agree with all this. But not enough people talk about real frameworks to go through the journey of whatever success looks like to you. The way I see it is that it's broken into 4 main things. Plan, act, reflect, and then pivot. Figure out what you want to achieve in x amount of time, this will probably change multiple times. Then break that into actionable steps, what can you do everyday to work towards that thing what are the inputs needed. Always measure inputs an easy example for this is physical. If you want to run a marathon in 6 months don't say, 3 months in run a half marathon that's output, say run 30 minutes everyday for 6 months. Once you figure out the inputs, do the work no matter how much you plan of you don't act it means nothing. Now these next two are the most important that isn't talked about enough. Reflect. Every week set your goals and at the end of the week see what you did and didn't. Talk about it, why didn't you do x, how did you feel doing y. You have to keep yourself accountable don't rely on social contracts. Then pivot. If a month in during one of your reflections you realize something isn't working, tweak it, do another month and see the difference. This is how businesses run and engineers crack problems. You're just taking it and applying it to your life
