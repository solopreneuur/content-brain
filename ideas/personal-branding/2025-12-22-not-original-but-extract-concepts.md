# Not original but extract concepts

Your "limits" are often just a lack of leverage.
Obviously you don't need to move like this to achieve your goal but Notice what happened in the scenario? The work didn't change.
- Focused work is still boring.
- The gym is still heavy.
The work stays the same.
But the suffering eased.
Why? Because when the "Why" is weak (e.g.,
"I want to look good"), your brain focuses on the friction (the cold, the tiredness).
When the "Why" is absolute (e.g., "I need to keep them safe"), your brain focuses on the outcome. The friction becomes irrelevant. It becomes just a toll you happily pay to get to the other side.

You don't need a tragedy. You just need to stop negotiating with your comfort.
