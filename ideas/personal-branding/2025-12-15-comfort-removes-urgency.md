# Comfort Removes Urgency

"People always say the biggest things holding you back are fear of failure and comfort. We've all heard that." "But what I think those actually come down to is this: when your life is set up so that not changing has no real consequences, your brain has no reason to take a risk." "That's why so many hyper-successful people come from hard environments. Not because suffering is magical — but because staying the same wasn't an option." "Most of us don't have tragic backstories. And thankfully, we don't need them. But modern life is designed so that if you do the bare minimum, you'll survive." "And survival is the enemy of urgency."
