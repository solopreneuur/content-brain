# Early 20s is when you make a choice.

Do you prescribe to college is the best 4 years of your life or do you try to make every year of your life better than the last.

You probably notice a lot of your classmates from high school starting to gain weight or get stuck in a dead end job.

Now ofc there's nuance it's called life and life circumstances.

But there's still a level of self accountability we should all have.

This is when talent stops carrying you. The super smart kids who aced exams because it was natural or the athletes that were gifted with good genetics and the average person.

Now everyone is at the same playing ground (more or less not counting outliers people from extreme poor families the hood etc).

At this point the only thing you can rely on is yourself how well you know yourself and the choices you make to move forward.
