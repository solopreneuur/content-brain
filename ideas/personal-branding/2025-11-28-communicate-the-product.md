# Communicate The Product

If you can communicate the product you can make money off the product. Look at GAGA. That video of ye is funny asl but what he's saying is actually very good advice. I think content creation is actually a great place to apply that thinking. When you make content you are the product. In this case communicating the product is bringing your true authentic self to your platform, your interests knowledge experiences your unique identity if you can communicate that in a cohesive way, you create your personal brand and over time you'll see patterns of what aspects of of you people resonate with the most and then you'll be like GAGA, brand deals, ads etc will flow easily to you at that point.
