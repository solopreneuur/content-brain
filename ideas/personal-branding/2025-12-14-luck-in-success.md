# Luck in Success

Let's talk about luck in success. I hate when people say someone got lucky, sure there are times where someone had a crazy advantage or unfair headset maybe they are blessed genetically or come from a rich family. But the default you should always assume is they worked for where they are even if it's not entirely true it helps your mental model of success, it's changing your view from pessimist to optimist, if you treat everyone the same and think they worked hard for something it makes you think it's possible for you too. I always used to say oh these people are just lucky they had a fast metabolism and I grew up fat. I remember the first week I got into running I saw a friend from high school before I even started I saw him running and thought damn I wanna get to that point one day because at that point I was dying after 0.5 miles. Literally two weeks later after I figured out pacing and breathing I was up to 2 miles. In that moment I tried to say I hope I get there instead of wow he's lucky he's always been fit and that self belief propelled me to that level so much faster than I ever imagined would happen.
