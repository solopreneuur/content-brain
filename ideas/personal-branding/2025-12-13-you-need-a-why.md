# You need a why

Why do you think in movies and and anime's they always show the character with a tragic back story. These people are literally sacrificing their lives on a daily basis to do whatever it is they do and it's because of the tremendous loss being the driving factor. This applies to our lives to on a different scale well I hope we don't have to go through those crazy things where an entire family is killed. But a why can come from many places loss or guilt or other negative emotions is just one way. It can also be from responsibility you see your mom working 3 jobs or your father coming home late every night exhausted and you realize that it's up to you now to make it. The point is wherever it comes from it should be in the back of your mind constantly so that you can push past the laziness and the doubt and remember the reason you are doing what you are doing.
