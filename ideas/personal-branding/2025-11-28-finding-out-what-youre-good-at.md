# Finding out what you're good at

Contrary to what I say in a lot of my videos I think the key is actually other people for once. The reason I say this is no matter how good of a grasp you have of yourself the level of self awareness you have it can never beat out a fresh perspective of you. Pay attention to what people compliment you on in all environments, work the gym just regular social settings. You may end up seeing a pattern in things and one day you realize wow I'm actually very good at x thing and I thought it was normal for people to be good at this but in reality I'm exceptional at it.
For me it was my spoken word my ability to convey complex ideas, I heard it from my coworkers when explaining some complex architecture, my friends when telling a story and even just random people I met. It all hit me when someone I normally never had a serious conversation with at work wanted advice on how to get promoted and she said "you speak eloquently "sometimes"", the sometimes is a glimpse into the nature of that relationship cause it was totally jokey where neither person took the other seriously and that's why it hit me harder and I just woke up one day and realized it.
