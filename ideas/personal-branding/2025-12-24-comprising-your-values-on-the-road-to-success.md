# Comprising your values on the road to success

There's this idea that to become successful you have to do anything it takes."  "Skip weddings. Alienate friends. Burn bridges. Cut corners."  "And to be fair — becoming successful does take a lot of effort, patience, and resilience."  "But I think there's a line people don't talk about."  "If the path you're on requires you to constantly compromise your values — lying, cheating, or becoming someone you don't respect — it usually backfires."  "Not because it's immoral."  "But because you end up chasing success for the wrong reasons, and it starts hollowing you out."  "Wanting it badly isn't the problem."  "Losing yourself in the process is."

The whole point is to evolve as a person that doesn't just mean make more money but also become a better human.
