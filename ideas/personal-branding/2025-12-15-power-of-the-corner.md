# Power of the Corner

"There's something powerful about being backed into a corner."  "Sometimes a 'worse' situation is actually better than being comfortably numb — because it forces action."  "This reminds me of a friend of mine. Everyone always called him dumb. He bought a $60k car at 22 while making minimum wage."  "On paper, it was a terrible decision."  "But the smartest thing he ever said was: 'It forces me to grind harder.'"  "And he was right. A few years later, he had a much higher-paying job and paid it off."  "Pressure isn't always bad.
