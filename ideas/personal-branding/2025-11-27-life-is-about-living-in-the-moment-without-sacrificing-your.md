# Life is about living in the moment without sacrificing your future

I myself have trouble with this and am working towards this on the daily. I think this whole thing of life is so much more nuanced than people say. On one hand you have the people that are the stoics the typical grind till you die type and the other are the free thinker live in the moment types. The balance you have to figure out how to strike is enjoying what you currently have and being grateful whilst still having goals and wanting to become someone new. It's not the journey or the destination it's who you become on the journey and enjoying each day and the process of doing so.
