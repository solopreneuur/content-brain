# Why cardio is so important on a cut

I'm sure you've heard people say this 1000 times but not many break it down logically. It's dead simple, your body and muscle mass can handle a lot more than you think they can, the issue arises when you're cardio doesn't match your body. Think about it if you can physically tolerate 30 minutes on the stair master but your lungs give out 10 minutes in every time you do stair master you're wasting 20 minutes of extra cardio which is extra calories you can burn. This is why you have to do cardio and I like running a couple times a week specifically for this because it really blows up your lung capacity.
