# The power of placebo

One thing that's always appealed me is when people will discredit or disregard something and say it's placebo. Like for example I started taking neurogum (this is not an ad LOL maybe in the future) and yk I def felt some differences in focus. Nothing crazy dramatic but when I looked back on the week I noticed some improvement fs. Anyway people are so quick to call something like neurogum or even like an ashwaganda placebo. But here's the thing, even if your brain is tricked into thinking the thing your taking is working as intended, then the thing is working. Like even if it's placebo if it works it works why does it matter if it's physiological making a change or your brain is tricked into it. At the end of the day the result is happening. I bet there's a connection with placebo and somehow tricking your brain to be productive but I haven't figured it out yet.
