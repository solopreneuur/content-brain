# Volume only accelerates results once feedback is being absorbed.

**Volume only accelerates results once feedback is being absorbed.**
**Before that, it just accelerates mistakes.**

One big lie I've noticed across a lot of things is that more volume automatically means faster results."  "People think if they just spam reps, spam work, spam output, things will click sooner."  "But that's not how most systems work."  "In the gym, training ten times a day doesn't build more muscle — it usually does the opposite because your body never adapts."  "I've noticed the same thing with content."  "If I post too much before I've learned from my mistakes, I'm not speeding up progress — I'm just wasting good ideas."  "Volume only helps once learning catches up.
