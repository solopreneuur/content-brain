# Momentum

Kobe had this saying it was have a dream sacrifice for it and never ever rest in the middle.

That last part is the thing that I see as being the most powerful.

It is so much easier to continue doing something that you've been doing than to stop and start.

The concept of momentum is so important.

NBA players who work in the off season so often  do so much better than the ones who chilled during the off season because they never broke out of the mode.
