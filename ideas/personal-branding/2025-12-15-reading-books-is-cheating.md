# Reading books is cheating

In school we read books because we are forced to many of us don't like it personally hated it it made me think I hated learning. The reason for reading was to pass a test. In real life there's no test to pass so many of us just stop reading. But the thing is is that reading is a cheat code. Someone more knowledgeable than you in the topic you want to learn about dedicated probably years to distill that information into those pages. You get to literally take all of that knowledge that you can apply to your own life for free by just spending a few weeks reading a book. You are literally cheating in life it's such an unfair edge.
