# Managing expectations

This is lowkey a kind of complicated and nuanced topic. The thing with managing expectations is like the line you teeter on is such a weird one. Cause look when you are pursuing something the best mentality to have ofc is an optimistic one right. Like yeah I will make 100k a month or I will blow up on TikTok. The thing is this is great when you start out but after yk you post 100 videos 200 videos and you see nothing you think maybe the people that doubted me were right or you start doubting yourself. This happens to us all I mean even as I'm making this  video when I first started I hoped I could get to ______. But still working on that yk. Butt the reason I'm not discouraged is before I even started I knew to set my expectations. Look I think that the key is to be overly optimistic but with the acknowledgment that whatever you want to do will just take much longer than you think. Because if you do truly believe and keep working it will happen, you just don't and really can't know exactly when. So when you're in the weeds just remember it will happen remember that optimism when you started keep it, but just know it'll be harder than you think.
