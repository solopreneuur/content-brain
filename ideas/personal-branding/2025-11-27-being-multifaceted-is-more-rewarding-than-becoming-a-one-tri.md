# Being multifaceted is more rewarding than becoming a one trick pony

I always say I hate the jack of all trades master of none quotes but they always miss the ending which is often better than master of one. The thing is though you can't do it all at once. The best examples of this are the people like da vinci. They didn't just become good at math and art and science and philosophy all together. They spent seasons of their life. Years of focus on one thing then the next and the next. One thing I've realized and I think many greats in history have to is everything is connected and feeds into each other, you might take a system or a way of thinking you learned while painting and apply it to science or business.
