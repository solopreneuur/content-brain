# The most simple psychological hack that helped me get my screen time down

Let's be honest in today's society one of the biggest hindrances from productivity is our phones and all the cheap dopamine accessible at our tips. Our parents were right it is that damn phone. Many people try all these fancy methods and it works for some like those apps that lock your screen time etc. That works for some people but then it becomes this toxic relationship where you're craving more but can't get it. Fundamentally you should naturally just start using your phone less subconsciously and it makes it much less limiting you're tricking yourself into freedom. It's literally just putting a widget on your Home Screen and in your right swipe (idk what it's called) with screen time. Every time you open up your phone even if you're not fixated on it you brain will see it and be like I shouldn't be over indulging. Over time your screen time naturally just goes down.
