# Survivorship Bias

Sure when it comes to specific actionable pieces of advice the successful person telling u what to do could just be wrong and have this bias. However there's no merit to thinking that luck Soley played a factor in that person becoming successful. At the end of the you have to act in whatever it is you want to do. I'll tell you this there's 100% chance you won't be successful if you don't act. Now if you act and still fail sure there's luck there.
