# Learning from other mediums and applying to yours

One thing I always preach is everything is connected in some way. Whatever you are currently doing even if it's not what you're going to do forever there will be at least 1 thing you learn (if you put effort in), that will help you on your next endeavor in life.

Nail the point of stuff being connected.

I think there's so much power in using other mediums to help influence your own lane because it creates this new unique fresh approach to things.

I'm doing this with hooks, sure I watch other creators and that's def a big thing I've been studying but I'm also looking at YouTube thumbnails and captions and news article headlines. These effectively all do the same thing right capture attention, but they are so different in audiences and like formats one might not think it's useful. But the hook I used for this video literally I learned from a news article I saw. (Do this)
