# Over Intellectualizing creativity

People with engineer math type brains tend to see everything as a system as an algorithm. This can be true in many instances from a reality grounded level even something like losing weight is an algorithm. There are very specific inputs that lead to the output. However reality is often more complicated than this, sure from a purely physical sense it is, but there's a mental and psychological aspect to it too. The human brain is far too complicated esp to someone who doesn't study it to try to crack with an algorithm.  This comes back to the topic of creativity and an observation I made of myself. There was times where I was hitting creative block with coming up with videos because I started drifting to my engineer brain I was like okay this is are the buckets my videos fall into I need this many in this I haven't come up with it. I would sit there trying to come up with stuff with no luck. The most success i had was writing whatever came to me with no constrains and then later putting into a bucket.

You should never go backwards with creativity let it flow in the rawest sense and then refine and inteelectualize it after.
