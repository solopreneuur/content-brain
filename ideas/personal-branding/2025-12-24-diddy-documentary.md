# Diddy Documentary

It showed this one concept so well. No matter how much money or power or external validation you have, it won't get rid of your internal insecurities.

Chasing after something and saying when I get this it'll make me happy or I'll feel better about myself when this happens clearly doesn't work.

Diddy literally achieved everything most people think about in terms of success he gets hits as an artist, is one of the most successful music execs of all time and gets with whatever woman he wants.

But none of that is enough for him he still continues to cheat lie and manipulate to get more. When is more enough, the answer is never because external success and validation will never make you whole.
