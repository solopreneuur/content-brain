# I lost 135 pounds with average genetics

Weight loss is not about genetics or predispositions to gaining weight or any of those things. Now before the scientific community attacks me what I am not saying is that these things are a non factor. Unfortunately some people have a much harder time than others when it comes to losing weight this is a fact. BUT like anything in life you play the cards you are delt. Take this example, someone who grows up with money has a much easier time becoming successful because they have more resources does that mean if you don't have that you just sit there and give up and blame your situation?? Life is unfair genetics are unfair deal with it. At the end of the day the same principle applies to every single person it's just a matter of how hard it is effort wise that genetics play a role in it's how many calories you burn vs how many you eat. If you have predispositions you may burn less than your peers. The other thing is like this whole community of oh my genetics are bad blah blah makes a lot of obese people think they have something wrong w them without actual evidence, before using it as an excuse to be lazy and fall into bad habits please actually get tests done to see if it's the reality. If it is then it sucks my mom has _______, but again same rules apply to her and she's down 30 pounds already. At the end of the day no matter the cards you are delt it comes back to mentality no it ands or buts.
