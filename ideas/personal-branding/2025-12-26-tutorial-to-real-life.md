# Tutorial to real life

Middle school High school even college are all the tutorial phase of life. At this point we are told what to do, whether it's our parents our teachers or our professors. The amount of autonomy in life is fairly limited. Our time is dictated by external factors.

The moment you step into the real world it's weird. It's an uncanny feeling of freedom.

For the first time ever majority of the choices we make are in our control.

Some of us go work others start a business etc etc.

The problem I've noticed in a lot of my peers is they still are stuck in that college mentality and refuse to take control of their life.

The key of transitioning from school to real life is being intentional.

Intentional in the decisions you make and rooted in an understanding of one self.
