# You shouldn't be hitting all your goals

A few (insert time span since new years) ago I was doing my yearly reflection for 2025.

I would say I had a solid year def did a decent bit of what I said.

But I didn't hit all my goals.

Now normally in previous years I would've been disappointed in myself.

But what changed my brain chemistry around this was the 70/30 rule at Amazon.

It's basically that you should be hitting 70% of your goals and failing 30% quarter after quarter.

Why?

It's simple really, if you're achieving 100% of your goals you aren't ambitious enough.

A simple analogy is this. If you do pushups and go for 10 you will probably do 10. But if you go for 50, you may end up at 35-40.

Same applies to ur goals u should be reaching slightly out.
