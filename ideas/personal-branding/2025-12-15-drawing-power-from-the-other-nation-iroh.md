# Drawing power from the other nation - iroh

I was thinking about this uncle iroh quote where he told Zuko to become truly powerful he needed to draw power from the other benders and understand their techniques and apply them back to fire bending.

I see this as true in terms of personality types. We hear all the time Type A Type B. The rigid structured people who like to plan vs the free thinkers who go with the flow.

Having friends or people you look up to or around you that are the opposite of what you are is so powerful.

For a Type A you like organization and structure but this can make you rigid and inflexible, taking inspiration from Type B can be so beneficial.

For Type B you like to be free and go with the flow but go too far and getting things done or having a schedule when it's necessary may be something you don't do. Type A's organization can help you channel your free thinking in a more focused way.
