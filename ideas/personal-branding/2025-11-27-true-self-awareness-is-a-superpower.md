# True Self Awareness is a superpower

Self awareness is something that is very lacking in society today. You know those nights where you randomly can't sleep and go wtf am I doing with my life, that's you being self aware but it's fleeting and you wake up the next day and eat that burger or indulge in that (insert bad thing) whatever it may be. That thought process you have at 1am can be easily replicated on a weekly even a daily basis if you'd like. It all comes down to having a period of under stimulation and being intentional with that time. The thoughts you're avoiding confront them. Write down everything and journal. Do a psychoanalysis of yourself. I've been feeling this way, why? I had this interaction and it made me crash out why? Did they say something that's an insecurity to me? Why is it an insecurity to me what's the issue at the root. At first it will suck because you might beat yourself up about certain things and maybe for some it won't be enough and you realize you truly need therapy but for many it will allow you to have mental clarity and focus on a daily basis.
