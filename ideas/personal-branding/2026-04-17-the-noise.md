# The noise

Starts with intense New York noise

Horns

People

Etc

And then just comes to a serene stillness

Talking about grappling being ambitious

Yet wanting to be grounded and not get caught up in the materialistic society

Where everyone is just running and stepping over other people

The epitome of America

New York City

The opportunities to better yourself exist like crazy

But how do you take advantage without getting lost in the sauce
