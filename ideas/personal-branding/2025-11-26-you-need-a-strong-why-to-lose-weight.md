# You need a strong why to lose weight

I can't replicate my situation from a mental standpoint for you I can't give you actionable tips on how it works from a physical perspective but in order to do those things behind it all is mentality. You need a strong why to do this because it's not easy. Maybe you got bullied growing up and never want to feel that way again maybe you're a man and got rejected because of the way you looked from being overweight maybe people said you will never be able to do it and you want to prove them wrong. Whatever it is you need to remember that every time you mess up and have that chocolate or that cheat day or miss that session you delay reaching your goal.
