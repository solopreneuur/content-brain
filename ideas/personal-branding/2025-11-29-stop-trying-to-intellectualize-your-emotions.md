# Stop trying to intellectualize your emotions

Now this is something that applies more to my typa a left brained individuals. You guys are probably engineers, data analysts this kind of archetype of person. As someone that falls into that category this is something I've struggled with. When you're this type of person your brain is oriented about everything being a problem that needs to be solved or an algorithm that needs to get cracked. With emotions sometimes you just have to let yourself feel it. There will be a root cause and a trigger but knowing that won't change the way you feel. In my own life an example of this is when I decided to leave everyone I knew and my family to move out to New York. It was something I felt sad about the first few weeks when I made the decision. In the beginning I tried fighting it and being like well x y and z why I should move why am I sad etc etc. but that didn't matter it didn't change the feeling of sadness in that moment I had to just let go and allow myself to feel sad and after a little while it went away and turned into excitement.
