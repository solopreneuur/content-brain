# Tyranny of Small Actions

Learned about it in my environmental policy class

If one person uses an extra couple toilet paper pieces doesn't affect the earth but when everyone does it the affects are catastrophic

Applies to your life

One bite isn't inherently bad but or one day of skipped work but stacked on top of years of doing it it becomes a much bigger issue more impactful

The way you do one thing is the way you do everything

I tried applying this an example is I was lazy with small habits like brushing throughly or eating deserts here and there but enough of those small mistakes throughout the day ended up just being a pattern of laziness that ended up affecting my work cut wtv
