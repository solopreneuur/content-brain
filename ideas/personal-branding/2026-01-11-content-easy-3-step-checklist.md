# Content easy 3 step checklist

You shouldn't overthink content and should just post

But there are 3 very easy things that make all the difference

When you stitched your audio together and about to post

Add captions research shows 60% of users watch with no audio sometimes, always have captions

Second step is do a silent video test -> Make sure caption sync, aren't too fast and there are no typos

Third easy thing is do an audio check, if you have to increase the volume on your phone speaker past 50% to hear clearly its too low, bump it up
