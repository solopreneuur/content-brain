# The ability to resist urges

The ability to resist your urges your id is such an essential part of lithe journey to becoming successful (or part of life). This ability is literally like a muscle u need to train and the moment you start fixing into your urges it's like a domino affect. I realized this driving home from the gym one night. I had gone on a run and was pretty hungry but almost out of calories. In the past I noticed that when I was in this hunger state where my brain is telling me go eat and I do it even a bit, then I start craving more and more. This is true in general across any bad habit. Some of you have probably heard Andrew hub Herman talk about it it's literally a part of your brain that grows bigger when u resist.
