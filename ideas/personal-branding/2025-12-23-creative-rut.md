# Creative Rut

The most simple 3 step framework to get out of a creative rut.

Reading because you are consuming other perspectives which blend with yours and give u a new original perspective.

Stepping away/taking a break, lets your brain process in the background it's like a PlayStation running in rest mode (maybe better analogy)

Changing environment, your brain subconsciously feels refreshed when you are in a new place (lots of studies on being in nature boosting creative process)
