# Success is different for everyone

Not everyone cares about becoming super wealthy or even having financial freedom. One thing in the entrepreneurship startup community I've noticed is many times people look down on others that work a 9-5. When I was in college I did this sometimes too. The thing is success isn't the same for everyone. Some people are more than happy if they get a secure check every two weeks have their kids college payed for and have a nice little house. To many that is enough. Now there are def times where people say that on the surface disguising laziness or fear of failure. Whatever it is we truly don't know what someone feels on the inside many times they don't even know but it's okay. There's no reason to judge everyone has their own journey just focus on yours.
