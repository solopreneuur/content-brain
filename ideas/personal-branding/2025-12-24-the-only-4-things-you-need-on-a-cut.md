# The only 4 things you need on a cut

There's soo much misinformation out there in the health space. People love to over complicate things with this fancy diet and insulin sensitivity studies and this and that.

The only 4 things that actually move the needle month after month that helped me lose 120 pounds.

Eating Whole Foods, this keeps you full and since your body is in a defecit it demands more nutrients this takes care of that.

Sleep, we all know this I don't need to explain.

Tracking your steps, this is such a cheat code and literally free calories you can burn or eat.

And the most important one is tracking your calories. In the beginning you absolutely have to do this and accurately. The first 6 months I ignored this and was like oh nah I can estimate, I grossly underestimated how many calories certain things had and it's just really hard to tell when you haven't tracked before.

That's it, no fancy diet, no ice bath at 5am just these simple things
