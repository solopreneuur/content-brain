# Self doubt is a ticking time bomb external doubt is a fire

"I think doubt is both a threat and a tool — it just depends on where it comes from."  "Internal doubt is dangerous. It sits in your head, makes you overthink, kills momentum, and slowly convinces you not to try."  "External doubt is different."  "When other people doubt me, I actually see it as fuel."  "Not in a loud, ego way — but in a quiet, long-term way."  "It reminds me that I'm doing something uncomfortable enough to be questioned."  "And I don't need to prove them wrong today or tomorrow."  "I just need to keep going long enough for reality to answer for me."  "That framing doesn't work for everyone, but for people wired like me, external doubt isn't discouraging — it's clarifying."
