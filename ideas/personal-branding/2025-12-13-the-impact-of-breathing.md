# The impact of breathing

People talk about this all the time but in practice it's actually so useful. I realized this one day I was working out in the gym w no music I could hear my breathing so I focused a lot harder on breathing in and out at the right time and I noticed so much less fatigue and literally was pushing more weight. This applies across life though dropping your cortisol in a stressful situation just breath, mediation box breathing etc I mean it makes sense right oxygen is literally the thing that gives us life .
