# Dopamine is a tool for momentum, not proof of progress.

Dopamine is one of the most powerful tools we have, but it's also kind of dangerous."  "It can help you build good habits, but it can just as easily lock in bad ones."  "One thing that's helped me a lot is simple checklists — even for small stuff."  "Checking off something dumb like oiling my hair or dermastamping gives me a small dopamine hit."  "And those little wins build momentum that makes bigger tasks feel less heavy."  "The important nuance though is this…"  "Those dopamine hits are meant to start work, not replace it."
