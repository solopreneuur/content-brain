# Keeping long term friendships as an adult

Be the one to reach out most people are lazy. They don't hate you or forgot about you but would rather just not make the effort to reach out. You have to be the one doing it that's just the case with many people. If you make the effort to reach out you will be able to sustain and not fall of with people for many many years all it takes is one text or one phone call.
