# Overthinking

Overthinking is a spectrum. We all suffer from it. For some it's about every small thing for others it's for the big stuff.

When we overthink something it's a storm of uncontrolled thoughts. This person pissed me off, I don't know how to do x or y.

The key is to control the storm.

The most simple thing that helped me overthink less was writing.

Write out the thing the decision the conversation. Putting it on paper forces coherent thought.

Once it's written it is so much easier to look at the situation clearly and with a fresh perspective and makes tackling the thing so much easier.

Just try it.
