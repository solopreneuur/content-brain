**EXPOSITION**

Back in January, I was sitting on a plane, sad as hell, watching the Chicago skyline get smaller and smaller.

I was leaving my family, my friends, everything.

But I told myself that if I just relentlessly worked on content here in New York, I could go back and retire my mom.

**CONFLICT**
9 months later I have jack shit to show for it

And I'm not scared of failing.

I'm scared of having to admit I was wrong.

To my brown parents who told me to focus on my job.

To my childhood friends who said I was making silly videos.

And to myself, that maybe I'm just not good enough.

**TURNING POINT**

And I genuinely couldn't figure out what I was doing wrong

**REALIZATION**

I've been so obsessed with hooks, and pacing, and the frame by frame of videos

And I may not get those things I missed back

But for the first time in nine months, I'm a step closer to accomplishing what I came out here for.  (Me walking shot, plane shot)
