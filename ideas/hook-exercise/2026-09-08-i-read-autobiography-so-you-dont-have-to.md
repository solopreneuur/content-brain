# I read ____ autobiography so you don't have to

Text hook should have the curiosity gap OR convey why it's applicable or both

Second sentence should deepen why this is relevant to them relate to them don't just say oh the person is rich that's dumb but use a trait they had that actually shows weakness or is relatable to my ICP
