# comics in the newspaper

Source: Bitter Builds (transcript)

A few days ago, a legendary entrepreneur said, "We don't go to the comic book store, but if the comics were in the newspaper, we'd read them." This quote genuinely shifted my perspective on how the next generation of millionaires is being made. 'Cause hear me out, Garfield was free in every newspaper, which is how millions came to recognize it. They made their billions from selling T-shirts and mugs. Like, the genius Gen Z entrepreneurs are not building comic book stores. They are simply appearing in the newspaper. The single greatest advantage is existing where all the attention lives, which is social media, and then building a brand to leverage it. 'Cause the thing is, nobody goes out of their way for a comic book store, but they won't skip it if it's in their hands
