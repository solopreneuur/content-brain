# identity priming

Source: Bitter Builds (transcript)

You ever notice how people who run talk about being runners, but people who try to run just talk about trying to go on runs? This is a concept called identity priming, and it's one of the largest indicators of future success. There was a Stanford study conducted with California voters, and there were two groups. Group number one, they asked how important is it to vote, and group number two, they asked how important is it to be a voter. That second group ended up voting at a much higher rate because there's an identity attached to being a voter. When you take identities away, actions become draining because they require willpower. This is the same reason that I don't beats I can't. I can't eat sugar is a rule someone's enforcing on you, while I don't eat sugar is just a description of the kind of person you are. Identify with who you wanna be and you'll actually get there quicker.
