# polymath hobbies nobel

Source: Bitter Builds (transcript)

Did you know Nobel Prize-winning scientists are 22 times more likely than average scientists to have artistic hobbies? Because breakthroughs happen when you collide two frames of reference that have never touched before. In 1912, Alexis Carrel won the Nobel Prize for figuring out how to sew blood vessels together. He learned the stitching pattern from lace-making. The thing is, if you only have one domain, you can only have ideas inside that one domain. A unique perspective depends on a wide breadth of knowledge, which you can only acquire through pursuing multiple interests. So I encourage you to stop seeing hobbies as a distraction and start seeing them as a part of your work
