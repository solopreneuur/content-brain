# mms habit swap

Source: Bitter Builds (transcript)

A famous entrepreneur stopped his alcoholism by replacing the alcohol with M&M's, and it shattered everything I thought I knew about habits. He says habits have three distinct stages, the cue, action, and reward. For example, if you're scrolling, the cue is boredom, the action is the scroll itself, and then the reward is the dopamine you receive. All you need to do to change a bad habit is switch out the action while keeping the cue and reward the same. In this guy's case, he just switched out the action of drinking with eating M&M's. This way it satisfies the cue of craving something to consume, and it gives a reward in the form of a sugar rush. This is by far the best way to stop a bad habit because you are essentially hijacking your brain, and that habit will disappear because the cue and reward are satisfied. But if you try to cut out all three cold turkey, it almost never works
