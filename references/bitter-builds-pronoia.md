# pronoia

Source: Bitter Builds (transcript)

All of your favorite successful people are delusional, but it's a very specific kind of delusion that you can replicate in your own life. Psychologists call it pronoia, which is literally just the opposite of paranoia, and what's so fascinating is they both refer to the same thing, the unfounded belief about other people's intentions toward you. Paranoia is the belief that the world is conspiring against you, but pronoia is the belief that the world is conspiring in your favor. This matters so much more than you think because the truth is you're likely good enough to get exactly what you want out of life. You're just not delusional enough to demand it for yourself
