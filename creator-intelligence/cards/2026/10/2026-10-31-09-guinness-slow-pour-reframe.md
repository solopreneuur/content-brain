# Guinness sold the wait as the point

**The find:** Drinkers complained that a pint of Guinness took about two minutes to pour, a two-part ritual left over from before the stout was nitrogenated. Instead of speeding it up, Guinness and its agency AMV built a campaign around the wait: "Good things come to those who wait", with ads like "Surfer".
**The surprise:** The brand advertised its own product's most annoying flaw, and the delay became proof of quality. When Guinness later tested a fast-pour tap, it kept the two-part pour at the heart of the brand.
**The idea:** [Reframing](../../../concepts/reframing.md). A weakness you can't fix can become the thing you sell, if you tell people what it means.
