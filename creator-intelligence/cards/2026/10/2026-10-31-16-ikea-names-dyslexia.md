# IKEA's names exist for a dyslexic founder

**The find:** IKEA's product names (Billy, Malm, Klippan) exist because founder Ingvar Kamprad was dyslexic and couldn't keep numerical product codes straight. He swapped the numbers for words, sorted by category: beds and wardrobes get Norwegian place names, rugs get Danish ones.
**The surprise:** One of the most recognisable naming systems in retail started as a workaround for one man's reading difficulty.
**The idea:** [Cognitive fluency](../../../concepts/cognitive-fluency.md). A name is easier to remember and talk about than a code, and a quirky system becomes part of the brand.
