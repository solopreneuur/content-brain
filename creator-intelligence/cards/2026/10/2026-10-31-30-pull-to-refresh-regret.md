# Pull-to-refresh's inventor regrets it

**The find:** Pull-to-refresh was invented by Loren Brichter, an ex-Apple engineer building Tweetie, a one-man Twitter app, around 2008. Years later he told The Guardian he never meant it to be addictive and that he regrets the downsides.
**The surprise:** A gesture from a small indie app became the slot-machine lever of nearly every feed, and its inventor became one of its critics.
**The idea:** [Variable rewards](../../../concepts/variable-rewards.md). A pull that sometimes brings something new and sometimes doesn't is the same schedule that makes slot machines hard to quit.
