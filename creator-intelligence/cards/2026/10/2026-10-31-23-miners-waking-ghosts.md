# Why miners saw ghosts underground

**The find:** In the 1970s, a Yorkshire miner napping in a dark tunnel woke to see a glowing miner in old-fashioned gear floating above him. Tim Harford's explanation: a hypnopompic experience, a waking dream in which the eyes are open but parts of the brain are still asleep, made likelier by darkness and silence.
**The surprise:** About one in ten people have had one on waking, and the brain areas most active are the ones that spot threats and human shapes, which is why the visions are so often an intruder.
**The idea:** [Hypnopompic hallucination](../../../concepts/hypnopompic-hallucination.md). What we expect shapes what we see: a miner raised on pit-ghost stories sees a dead miner, not a dream.
