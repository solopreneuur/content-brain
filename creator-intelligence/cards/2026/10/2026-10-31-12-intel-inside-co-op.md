# Intel paid for other brands' ads

**The find:** Why did so many 1990s PC ads carry the Intel Inside logo? Intel put a share of every chip purchase into a co-op fund that paid up to half the cost of a PC maker's ads, as long as the ad carried the logo. By the end of 1992, over 500 manufacturers had signed up.
**The surprise:** Customers never see a processor, and Intel made them ask for one by name by paying its own customers to advertise it. The scheme reportedly cost Intel around $2 billion in its first decade.
**The idea:** [Ingredient branding](../../../concepts/ingredient-branding.md). Make an invisible component a reason to buy the finished product, and the brand that sells the box can't easily swap you out.
