# Two free stamps nearly doubled car washes

**The find:** A car wash handed out 300 loyalty cards. Half needed 8 stamps for a free wash; half needed 10 but came with 2 already stamped. Same 8 washes either way, but 19% finished the first card and 34% finished the second.
**The surprise:** Identical effort, nearly double the completion, just because the second card looked like it had already started.
**The idea:** The [endowed progress effect](../../../concepts/endowed-progress-effect.md). People push harder towards a goal they think they've already begun, even when the head start is a gift.
