# Why ATMs return your card first

**The find:** Most ATMs make you take your card before they hand over the cash. Machines that did it the other way round saw people walk off with their money and leave their cards behind. One study found card-first was at least 22% faster and produced no lost cards.
**The surprise:** The order is less about security than about attention: once you have what you came for, your brain stops paying attention.
**The idea:** [Post-completion error](../../../concepts/post-completion-error.md). Once the main goal is done, people forget the clean-up step (the original in the photocopier, the cap on the petrol tank), so good design puts the last step before the reward.
