# Seeing the cooks made food taste better

**The find:** Researchers set up video screens in a cafeteria so diners and cooks could see each other. When both sides could see, diners rated the food 22% higher and the cooks served 19% faster.
**The surprise:** The food didn't change. Being seen made the cooks try harder, and seeing the effort made diners value the meal more.
**The idea:** The [labor illusion](../../../concepts/labor-illusion.md). People value what they can see being worked on, and workers try harder for people they can see.
