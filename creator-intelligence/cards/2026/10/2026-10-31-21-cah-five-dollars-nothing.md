# 11,000 people paid $5 for nothing

**The find:** On Black Friday 2015, Cards Against Humanity offered a deal: pay $5 and get nothing. 11,248 people paid, some more than once, and one paid $100, for a total of $71,145. The company split it among its staff.
**The surprise:** On the biggest discount day of the year, the best seller was nothing at full price.
**The idea:** [Identity signalling](../../../concepts/identity-signalling.md). People paid to be in on the joke, because joining showed they were the kind of person who gets it.
