# "Learn More" beat "Sign Up" for Obama

**The find:** The Obama campaign's splash page tested its button wording. "Learn More" signed up about 8.9% of visitors against about 7.5% for the original "Sign Up". With the image and button tests that followed, sign-ups rose by more than 40%, worth an estimated $60 million in donations.
**The surprise:** The button that asked for less commitment got more commitment.
**The idea:** [Friction](../../../concepts/friction.md). "Sign up" feels like a decision; "learn more" feels like a step. Lower the perceived cost of the first click and more people reach the real ask.
