# Kozmo delivered in an hour in 1999

**The find:** Kozmo.com delivered films, snacks and ice cream across New York within an hour, free, with no minimum order. It raised about $280 million, including $60 million from Amazon, and agreed to pay Starbucks $150 million for video-return boxes in its cafés. It shut down in April 2001.
**The surprise:** The model behind today's quick-delivery apps was running, and failing, two decades earlier.
**The idea:** [Right idea, wrong time](../../../concepts/right-idea-wrong-time.md). Customers loved Kozmo; the cost of delivering a single pint of ice cream killed it, long before smartphones and dense order data could make the sums work.
