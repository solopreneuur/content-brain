# Amazon's algorithm that raised rivals' prices

**The find:** The FTC's lawsuit against Amazon describes "Project Nessie", an algorithm that found products where rival stores copied Amazon's prices. Amazon raised its price on those items, competitors followed, and the high price held. The FTC says it made Amazon over $1 billion; Amazon says the FTC mischaracterises the tool.
**The surprise:** The famous low-price retailer allegedly used rivals' price-matching bots against them, to push prices up, not down.
**The idea:** [Price leadership](../../../concepts/price-leadership.md). When competitors follow your price automatically, you set the market's price, in either direction.
