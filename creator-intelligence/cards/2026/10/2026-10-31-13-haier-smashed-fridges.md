# Haier's CEO smashed 76 fridges

**The find:** In 1985, after a customer returned a faulty fridge, Haier's new boss Zhang Ruimin checked the stock in the Qingdao factory and found 76 defective units. Fridges were scarce and expensive, but he lined them up and had them smashed with sledgehammers, swinging the first blow himself.
**The surprise:** In a shortage economy where even a faulty fridge would sell, destroying stock looked insane. Haier became the world's biggest appliance maker, and one of the hammers is on display at its headquarters.
**The idea:** [Quality signalling](../../../concepts/quality-signalling.md). A costly, public act sets a standard that a memo never could.
