# Häagen-Dazs is fake Danish from the Bronx

**The find:** Häagen-Dazs was founded by Reuben Mattus, a Polish-Jewish immigrant selling ice cream in the Bronx. He invented the name to sound Danish, though "ä" and "zs" don't exist in Danish, and printed a map of Denmark on the cartons. He admitted the name meant nothing.
**The surprise:** One of the world's best-known "European" luxury brands was a made-up word from New York, chosen partly to honour Denmark for rescuing its Jews in the Second World War.
**The idea:** [Foreign branding](../../../concepts/foreign-branding.md). A foreign-sounding name signals craft and quality before anyone tastes the product.
