# Prius drivers pay to look green

**The find:** The Honda Civic Hybrid was about as green as the Prius but looked like an ordinary Civic. Economists Steven and Alison Sexton estimated that buyers paid between $430 and $4,200 extra for the Prius, whose odd shape announced "hybrid" to everyone on the street.
**The surprise:** People weren't only paying for fuel economy. They were paying for the shape that showed it off.
**The idea:** [Conspicuous conservation](../../../concepts/conspicuous-conservation.md): the green version of conspicuous consumption. Make the virtuous choice visible and people will pay more for it.
