# Costco caps its own markup at 14%

**The find:** Costco co-founder Jim Sinegal set a rule that no branded item is marked up more than 14%, and no own-label item more than 15%, even when shoppers would happily pay more. A Deutsche Bank analyst complained that Costco was better at serving members and employees than shareholders.
**The surprise:** Wall Street called the cap money left on the table. Costco has kept it for decades, and its stock has far outrun the market.
**The idea:** [Self-imposed constraint](../../../concepts/self-imposed-constraint.md). Sinegal warned that going to 16% or 18% would start a slide; a hard rule protects discipline, and customers' trust, better than case-by-case judgement.
