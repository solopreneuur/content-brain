# Tall avatars made people negotiate harder

**The find:** Stanford researchers gave people taller or shorter avatars in virtual reality and had them split money with a stranger. People in taller bodies proposed more unfair splits in their own favour, and a follow-up found the effect carried into face-to-face negotiation afterwards.
**The surprise:** Nobody told them to act differently. They just looked down at a body that wasn't theirs and became that person.
**The idea:** The [Proteus effect](../../../concepts/proteus-effect.md). We infer who we are from how we look, even in a digital body, and then act the part.
