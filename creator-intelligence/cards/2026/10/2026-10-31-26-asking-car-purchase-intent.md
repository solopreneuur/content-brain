# Asking about a car sold more cars

**The find:** In a study of more than 40,000 people, simply asking whether they planned to buy a new car in the next six months raised their purchase rate by 35%. Asking about personal computers had the same kind of effect.
**The surprise:** The survey was meant to measure behaviour, and it changed it.
**The idea:** The [mere-measurement effect](../../../concepts/mere-measurement-effect.md), also called the question-behaviour effect. Asking someone if they'll do something makes the option more present in their mind, so they become more likely to do it.
