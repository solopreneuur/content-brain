# Mark Cuban: everyone's tied for second

**The find:** Billionaire and former Shark Tank investor Mark Cuban says his first fortune came from one rule: with any new software, "there was only going to be one person who understood any given software better than me, and that would be the person who wrote it... if I busted my ass, I was tied for second." He says the same now holds for AI: "There's the creator and everybody else is tied for second."
**The surprise:** A billionaire puts his edge down to staying up all night reading software manuals, not to money, contacts or genius.
**The idea:** [Learning-curve advantage](../../../concepts/learning-curve-advantage.md). When a tool is brand new, nobody has a head start, so whoever puts in the hours first leads everyone except the people who built it.
