# "Good luck" nearly tripled job fair turnout

**The find:** A UK jobcentre texted jobseekers about a recruitment event. The plain text got about 10% to turn up. Adding their name, the adviser's name, "I've booked you a place" and "Good luck" raised attendance to about 27%.
**The surprise:** Two words of goodwill and a sense that someone had done them a favour nearly tripled turnout. It cost nothing.
**The idea:** [Reciprocity](../../../concepts/reciprocity.md). When someone has done something for you, even booking a seat, you feel you owe them a show. The warmth also lifts morale in people who expect rejection.
