# Mondays and birthdays send people to the gym

**The find:** Google searches for "diet" spike at the start of every week, month and year, and university gym visits rise at the start of a week, a month, a semester and after a birthday. The one exception: students went to the gym less after turning 21.
**The surprise:** An arbitrary date on a calendar measurably changes behaviour, and so does a birthday, unless it's the one that comes with a legal drink.
**The idea:** The [fresh start effect](../../../concepts/fresh-start-effect.md). Dates that feel like a new chapter let people separate from their past failures, so they're the best moment to ask for a new habit.
